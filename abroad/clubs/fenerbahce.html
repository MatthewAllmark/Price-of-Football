How tourists can buy

Süper Lig tickets are sold through Passo, and you need a Passo (Passolig) fan card. Some clubs only sell to holders of a card branded with their logo. Full Turkey guide →

  • Sales only via Passo; from 24 hours before kick-off only holders of a Fenerbahçe-branded Passolig card can buy. Tickets are non-transferable. Fenerbahçe SK ticket announcement (28 Oct 2026) checked 2 Nov 2026

UK government travel advice

  • It is illegal not to carry some form of photographic ID in Turkey. Always carry your passport or residence permit. In some busy areas, especially Istanbul, the authorities may stop people for ID checks. There are also several police checkpoints on main roads across Turkey. Cooperate with officials conducting checks.
