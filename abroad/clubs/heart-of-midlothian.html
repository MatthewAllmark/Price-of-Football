It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or connected to any club, league or ticket seller. Club names are used for identification only, and we do not use club badges or logos.

Every price, capacity, rivalry and atmosphere note links to its source with the date we checked it. If we could not find a figure from a source we trust, we say “not published”; we never estimate. We do not show live prices or live sold-out status. Ticket links go only to official club or league ticketing. We never link to resale or betting sites.
