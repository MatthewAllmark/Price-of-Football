How tourists can buy

Italian tickets are named to you and you need photo ID to buy and to get in. Some games are limited to club card (Fidelity Card) holders, and police can ban sales to people living in certain areas. Full Italy guide →

  • Club fidelity card costs €20 online and lasts three years; sale phases give priority to card holders. SSC Napoli ticket info checked 2 Nov 2026

UK government travel advice

  • Under Italian law, police can stop you at any time to carry out an identity check or your date of entry into the country. Carry photo ID with you at all times. The police will normally ask for your full passport if you are stopped while driving.
