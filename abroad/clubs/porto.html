How tourists can buy

Clubs sell to members (sócios) first and then to the general public, usually through the club's own ticket site or the SmartFan / 2ticket platforms linked on club pages. Full Portugal guide →

  • For the Clássico v Benfica (Sept 2026), general public had only two categories: €93 central and €160 central nascente premium; cheaper tiers (from €20) were members-only. Ordinary league matches are cheaper. O Jogo: "FC Porto: bilhetes para o clássico com o Benfica chegam aos 160 euros" checked 5 Nov 2026

UK government travel advice

  • You must provide photo ID if asked by a police officer. The police have the right to hold you at a police station until they have confirmed your identity. It is mandatory to carry ID when driving.
