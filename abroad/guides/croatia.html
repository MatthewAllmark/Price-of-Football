Tickets as a tourist › Croatia

Football tickets in Croatia for tourists

HNL clubs sell online through their own ticketing sites, but some stands or sales are limited to members.

What the official sources say

  • For some Hajduk stands you must have valid club membership before you buy. Lika Online checked 2 Nov 2026
  • Dinamo Zagreb members get free tickets for four league or cup matches a year, but not for derbies. GNK Dinamo tickets checked 2 Nov 2026

UK government (FCDO) advice

  • Large gatherings and public events are overwhelmingly safe and peaceful in Croatia, including sporting events. However, there are periodic cases of violence between football fans around matches. Care should be taken near football stadiums around match times, especially “derby” matches, and you should:
  • Always carry your passport or, if you’re a resident, your Croatian ID card. Keep a copy of your passport’s photo page in a safe place.

FCDO travel advice: Croatia (updated 11 Aug 2026, checked 2 Nov 2026)

Matchday Abroad is an independent guide for UK fans who want to see a local match on holiday. It is part of Price of Football. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or connected to any club, league or ticket seller. Club names are used for identification only, and we do not use club badges or logos.

Every price, capacity, rivalry and atmosphere note links to its source with the date we checked it. If we could not find a figure from a source we trust, we say “not published”; we never estimate. We do not show live prices or live sold-out status. Ticket links go only to official club or league ticketing. We never link to resale or betting sites. Fixture dates can change, so always confirm on the club’s official site before you travel.
