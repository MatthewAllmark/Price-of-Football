Tickets as a tourist › Germany

Football tickets in Germany for tourists

Bundesliga clubs give season-ticket holders and members first access to single-match tickets. Any left over go on wider sale in the club's online shop.

What the official sources say

  • Leverkusen opened its exclusive sale to season-ticket holders and club members before any wider sale. Bayer 04 ticket news checked 2 Nov 2026
  • Borussia Dortmund publishes a 2026/27 day-ticket price list (standing €18.50; seats €35–95) with a surcharge for top games. BVB ticket prices checked 2 Nov 2026

UK government (FCDO) advice

  • It’s not a legal requirement to carry ID. However, if you cannot show ID when asked, police can escort you to get your passport.

It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or connected to any club, league or ticket seller. Club names are used for identification only, and we do not use club badges or logos.

Every price, capacity, rivalry and atmosphere note links to its source with the date we checked it. If we could not find a figure from a source we trust, we say “not published”; we never estimate.
