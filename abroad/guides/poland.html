What the official sources say

  • Lech Poznań says its fan card (Karta Kibica) is not needed for its matches. Tickets can be printed or held on a phone. Lech Poznań ticketing checked 2 Nov 2026

UK government (FCDO) advice

  • Always carry ID. A printed copy of the photo page of your passport is acceptable.

Matchday Abroad is an independent guide for UK fans who want to see a local match on holiday. It is part of Price of Football. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or connected to any club, league or ticket seller. Club names are used for identification only, and we do not use club badges or logos.

Every price, capacity, rivalry and atmosphere note links to its source with the date we checked it. If we could not find a figure from a source we trust, we say “not published”; we never estimate. We do not show live prices or live sold-out status. Ticket links go only to official club or league ticketing. We never link to resale or betting sites. Fixture dates can change, so always confirm on the club’s official site before you travel.
