Tickets as a tourist › Netherlands

Football tickets in Netherlands for tourists

Dutch clubs sell mainly online through their own ticket shops, often only to registered fans or holders of a club card.

What the official sources say

  • Ajax sells only through its official Ticketshop. The 2026/27 Ajax Club Card (€5) gives access to sales and priority, and is compulsory for some matches. Ajax: losse kaartverkoop 2026/2027 checked 2 Nov 2026
  • At Fortuna Sittard you can buy online in advance, or at the matchday ticket windows if no restrictions apply. Fortuna Sittard tickets checked 2 Nov 2026

UK government (FCDO) advice

  • Anyone aged 14 or over must always be able to show identification. British nationals should use their passport as ID. Dual nationals can show a Dutch driving licence, Dutch passport or identity card.
