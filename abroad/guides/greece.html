Tickets as a tourist › Greece

Football tickets in Greece for tourists

Every Super League ticket is tied to the Gov.gr Wallet app, and big clubs also require their own fan card. Some clubs ask for Greek ID numbers that visitors do not have.

What the official sources say

  • Entry to the stadium is only with the Gov.gr Wallet app. Olympiacos FC ticket notice checked 2 Nov 2026
  • Fans without Greek residency verify the ticket in Gov.gr Wallet by choosing 'Non Greek Resident'. Aris FC ticket notice checked 2 Nov 2026
  • Levadiakos requires your AMKA (Greek social-security number) and police ID number for every ticket. Levadiakos FC ticket notice checked 2 Nov 2026
  • For big games Olympiacos requires its 2026/27 fan card (€15 or €20); PAOK requires its €20 season card. Gazzetta: Olympiacos v PAOK tickets checked 2 Nov 2026

UK government (FCDO) advice

  • Always carry your passport, to show to the police if they request identification. Police may not accept a copy.
