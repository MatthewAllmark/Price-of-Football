Tickets as a tourist › Spain

Football tickets in Spain for tourists

Most LaLiga and second-tier clubs sell match tickets online on their own ticketing sites once members have had priority. Tickets are named to the holder.

What the official sources say

  • Tickets are personal ('nominativas') and the named holder cannot be changed. CA Osasuna ticket notice checked 2 Nov 2026
  • Clubs may ask you to prove your identity at the turnstile. CD Tenerife ticket notice checked 2 Nov 2026
  • Real Madrid publishes separate prices for members and the general public; the general public paid €75–€235 for the 2026/27 home opener. Mundo Deportivo, 17 Aug 2026 checked 2 Nov 2026

UK government (FCDO) advice

  • You must provide photo ID if asked by a police officer. This includes the Guardia Civil and national, regional and local police forces. The police have the right to hold you at a police station until they have confirmed your identity.

Matchday Abroad is an independent guide for UK fans who want to see a local match on holiday. It is part of Price of Football. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or connected to any club, league or ticket seller. Club names are used for identification only, and we do not use club badges or logos.

Every price, capacity, rivalry and atmosphere note links to its source with the date we checked it. If we could not find a figure from a source we trust, we say “not published”; we never estimate. We do not show live prices or live sold-out status. Ticket links go only to official club or league ticketing. We never link to resale or betting sites. Fixture dates can change, so always confirm on the club’s official site before you travel.
