Tickets as a tourist › Italy

Football tickets in Italy for tourists

Italian tickets are named to you and you need photo ID to buy and to get in. Some games are limited to club card (Fidelity Card) holders, and police can ban sales to people living in certain areas.

What the official sources say

  • Tickets are personal under the Interior Ministry decree of 6 June 2005, so you need valid ID when you buy. Atalanta: info biglietteria checked 2 Nov 2026
  • For some matches, for public-order reasons, tickets can be sold only to holders of the club's Fidelity Card. Atalanta: info biglietteria checked 2 Nov 2026
  • Sales can be restricted by place of residence. For Cagliari v Frosinone, residents of Sardinia were banned from buying in the away sector. Cagliari Calcio ticket notice checked 2 Nov 2026
  • Bologna asks for an original ID document showing your residence when you buy and when you enter. Bologna FC match ticket info checked 2 Nov 2026
  • Napoli sells in phases that end with an open sale ('vendita libera'). Its Fidelity Card costs €20 online and lasts three years. SSC Napoli ticket info checked 2 Nov 2026

UK government (FCDO) advice

  • Under Italian law, police can stop you at any time to carry out an identity check or your date of entry into the country. Carry photo ID with you at all times. The police will normally ask for your full passport if you are stopped while driving.
