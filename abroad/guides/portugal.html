Tickets as a tourist › Portugal

Football tickets in Portugal for tourists

Clubs sell to members (sócios) first and then to the general public, usually through the club's own ticket site or the SmartFan / 2ticket platforms linked on club pages.

What the official sources say

  • Benfica lists ticket prices for members and for the general public ('público') for its home league games. SL Benfica tickets checked 2 Nov 2026
  • Some sales are members-only: Vitória SC's away tickets for its match at Benfica were sold exclusively to Vitória members. Vitória SC ticket notice checked 2 Nov 2026
  • Famalicão required a member's card to enter the stadium for its home match against Arouca, so check each match notice. FC Famalicão ticket notice checked 2 Nov 2026

UK government (FCDO) advice

  • You must provide photo ID if asked by a police officer. The police have the right to hold you at a police station until they have confirmed your identity. It is mandatory to carry ID when driving.

Matchday Abroad is an independent guide for UK fans who want to see a local match on holiday. It is part of Price of Football. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or connected to any club, league or ticket seller. Club names are used for identification only, and we do not use club badges or logos.

Every price, capacity, rivalry and atmosphere note links to its source with the date we checked it. If we could not find a figure from a source we trust, we say “not published”; we never estimate. We do not show live prices or live sold-out status. Ticket links go only to official club or league ticketing. We never link to resale or betting sites. Fixture dates can change, so always confirm on the club’s official site before you travel.
