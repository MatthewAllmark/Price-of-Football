Tickets as a tourist › Turkey

Football tickets in Turkey for tourists

Süper Lig tickets are sold through Passo, and you need a Passo (Passolig) fan card. Some clubs only sell to holders of a card branded with their logo.

What the official sources say

  • Everyone buying needs a Passo card. You can buy at the box office, passo.com.tr or the Passo app, up to two tickets per person. Beşiktaş JK ticket notice checked 2 Nov 2026
  • From 24 hours before kick-off, only holders of a Fenerbahçe-branded Passolig card can buy, and tickets cannot be transferred. Fenerbahçe SK ticket notice checked 2 Nov 2026
  • Rizespor home tickets can only be bought with a Rizespor-branded Passo card. Çaykur Rizespor ticket notice checked 2 Nov 2026

UK government (FCDO) advice

  • It is illegal not to carry some form of photographic ID in Turkey. Always carry your passport or residence permit. In some busy areas, especially Istanbul, the authorities may stop people for ID checks. There are also several police checkpoints on main roads across Turkey. Cooperate with officials conducting checks.

FCDO travel advice: Turkey (updated 27 Oct 2026, checked 2 Nov 2026)
