Tickets as a tourist › Argentina

Football tickets in Argentina for tourists

Big Argentine clubs sell mainly to members (socios). Non-members can usually get in only as a member's guest, or through limited public sales at some clubs.

What the official sources say

  • Racing sells 'Invitado' (guest) tickets for non-members, priced higher than members' tickets. Racing Club: Entradas checked 2 Nov 2026
  • Boca Juniors lists a 'Socio Internacional' membership category for people living outside Argentina (published fee US$13). Boca Juniors: socios – valores checked 2 Nov 2026
  • At Huracán, non-members can buy platea or popular tickets online. Entry is by facial recognition, the app QR code or a physical DNI. CA Huracán ticket notice checked 2 Nov 2026

UK government (FCDO) advice

The FCDO page has no football-specific advice for this country.
