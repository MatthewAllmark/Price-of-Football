About & methodology

Matchday Abroad helps UK fans find a local match on holiday: who is playing, how big it is likely to feel, how tourists can get in, and roughly what it costs. Every figure has a source and the date we checked it.

Our rules

  • We never estimate. If a price, capacity or fact is not published by a source we trust, we say “not published”.
  • Ticket links go only to the club’s official ticketing page, the club site, or the league’s official platform (Passo in Turkey). We never link to resale or betting sites.
  • No live prices and no live sold-out status. We show a typical adult price range from an official price list or reputable press, with the match it came from.
  • No club badges or logos.

The atmosphere score (0–10)

Each fixture gets a score that adds up three parts. The breakdown is shown on every fixture.

score = derby (0–4) + stakes (0–3) + home fans (0–3)

derby = 4 if our source names the derby (e.g. “Derby della Capitale”)
= 3 if the source lists the rivalry without a name
= 0 otherwise

stakes = table part + late-season bonus, capped at 3
table part (largest that applies), from the current league table:
both teams within 6 pts of the leader ........ 2.0
one in that title race, other in top places ... 1.5
one team in the title race .................... 1.0
relegation six-pointer (both in/near zone) .... 1.5
both in the top ⌈N/4⌉+1 places ................ 1.0
one relegation-threatened side ................ 0.75
one side in the top ⌈N/4⌉+1 places ............ 0.5
“near the relegation zone” = bottom 3 places (bottom 2 in leagues of 12 or fewer),
or within 3 pts of the top relegation place
late-season bonus = 0 before half-way, 0.5 from half-way, 1.0 from three-quarters

home fans = (our 1–5 rating − 1) × 0.75; unrated clubs score 0

Ratings from 1 to 5 are our editorial judgement. We only give one when we can quote a published source describing the crowd, and the quote and link are on the club page. 46 clubs are rated so far. The stakes part uses the league tables checked on 2 Nov 2026, so it will drift as the season moves on.

For this release we checked every fixture against the league’s or FA’s own site where one is publicly readable, and we record the source and check date on each match. Each fixture carries one of three labels:

  • confirmed — the official source shows both the date and the kick-off time.
  • date confirmed, time not set — the official source shows the date, but the league has not yet set the kick-off time.
  • provisional — the date and time come from a public scoreboard feed and have not yet been checked against the league site.

Kick-off times are shown in the stadium’s own time zone, labelled on each card. Where a league page prints times in the viewer’s time zone we converted them from that, rather than assuming they were local. Fixture counts by label: 212 confirmed, 132 date-only and 212 provisional.

Clubs, stadiums, capacity and rivals

Capacities come from each league’s Wikipedia season article (stadium table), cited to the exact revision. Montenegro capacities come from the stadium or club article infobox. Stadium coordinates come from Wikipedia or Wikidata. Rivalries come from Wikipedia’s derby and rivalry list articles, also cited to the revision. Official websites come from Wikidata, and ticket pages were found on club sites.

Prices and exchange rates

Typical prices come from official club price lists and ticket announcements, or reputable national and regional press. Pounds are a conversion, rounded to the nearest pound, at:

  • EUR: €1 = £0.8358 – ECB euro reference rates (2 Nov 2026)
  • PLN: €1 = 4.4185 zł and €1 = £0.8358 (cross rate) – ECB euro reference rates (2 Nov 2026)
  • TRY: €1 = ₺56.0442 and €1 = £0.8358 (cross rate) – ECB euro reference rates (2 Nov 2026)
  • RSD: £1 = 140.7854 dinars (NBS middle rate) – National Bank of Serbia middle rate (2 Nov 2026)
  • ARS: £1 = ARS 2,053.57 (BCRA quote of 30 Oct 2026, latest published on 2 Nov) – Banco Central de la República Argentina, exchange quotes (30 Oct 2026)

Albanian lek prices would use the Bank of Albania rate, but we found no published lek ticket prices.

What is not covered yet

  • Fixtures are now listed for Albania, Croatia, Poland and Argentina from their official league or FA pages, and for part of LaLiga Hypermotion from LaLiga’s own results page. Still missing: Serbia, Primera Federación and Liga Portugal 2, where no publicly readable official fixture list was available. A paid feed such as API-Football would cover them. Its pricing page lists Free ($0, 100 requests/day), Pro ($19/month), Ultra ($29/month) and Mega ($39/month).
  • Serie A, the Eredivisie, the Primeira Liga and Super League Greece were only available to us as public scoreboard feeds, not from a machine-readable official page, so their fixtures stay marked provisional. Serie A’s and the Eredivisie’s calendar pages render one round at a time and expose no stable per-round URL; the Greek league site blocks automated requests.
  • 102 fixtures still have no kick-off time because the league has not published one yet.
  • Prices are published for 48 of 214 clubs. Many clubs only show prices inside their ticket shop once a match goes on sale.
  • Kick-off times for many fixtures are not yet confirmed by the leagues.
