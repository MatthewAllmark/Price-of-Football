Cheapest season tickets from the Premier League to the National League

Pick a league and sort the “Season ticket from” column to rank clubs by their cheapest adult season ticket for 2026/27. Many clubs sell a handful of restricted, terrace or family-area places below their usual entry price, and lower-league clubs often quote early-bird or renewal prices, so read each club’s notes before comparing.

Away tickets: the £30 cap is Premier League only

Adult away tickets at Premier League grounds are capped at £30 until at least the end of 2027/28. There is no cap in the EFL or the National League: the away price is whatever the home club charges, and we show it only where we found it from the club or a source we trust.

Price of a pint and a pie at every ground

Premier League pint and pie prices come from a club-by-club press survey. We haven’t found a reputable 2026/27 survey for the EFL or National League, so below the Premier League we only show a pint, pie or programme price where a club’s own guide (or, labelled “other”, a dated fan guide) lists one — most of those cells are still blank.
