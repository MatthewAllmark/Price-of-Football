About Price of Football & our methodology

Price of Football is an independent, unaffiliated database of what it costs to watch UK football. Version 1 covers the 20 Premier League clubs for the 2026/27 season.

Independent and unaffiliated

We are not connected to, sponsored by or endorsed by the Premier League, the EFL, any club, any ticket seller or any brewer. Club and competition names are used only to identify them. We deliberately don’t use club badges, crests or logos.

Where the numbers come from

  1. Club websites first — official ticketing pages, price lists and announcements.
  2. Reputable press second — national and local newspapers (e.g. BBC Sport, The Mirror, Manchester Evening News, Liverpool Echo, Birmingham Mail, local titles) and GiveMeSport, where the club’s own figures couldn’t be read.
  3. Other sources only when labelled — a small number of figures come from fan sites, a ticket-guide site or a programme-collector site. These are tagged on each club page so you can judge them.

Stadium names and capacities are taken from the Wikipedia 2026–27 Premier League article (which cites club sources). Pint and pie prices come from a single press survey in which the newspaper contacted every club, so they’re comparable but are not club-published price lists.

Rules we follow

  • Every figure has a source link and the date we last checked it.
  • If we can’t find a figure from a source we trust, we show “—”. We never estimate, interpolate or guess.
  • Where sources disagree we use the club’s own figure if we can read it, otherwise the most specific press report, and we say so in the note.
  • Prices are adult, general admission and exclude hospitality unless the note says otherwise.

What each column means

  • Cheapest adult season ticket: Cheapest adult season ticket the club sells for home league games (see note for area/product).
  • Dearest adult season ticket: Most expensive adult general-admission season ticket (hospitality excluded where identifiable).
  • Cheapest adult matchday ticket: Cheapest adult single-match ticket for a league game (category/grade in note).
  • Dearest adult matchday ticket: Most expensive adult general-admission single-match ticket for a league game (category/grade in note).
  • Away ticket (league cap): Price paid by away fans (Premier League cap).
  • Pint of lager: Price of a pint of lager inside the ground.
  • Pie: Price of a pie inside the ground.
  • Matchday programme: Cover price of the matchday programme.

Known limitations

  • Several clubs publish prices only as images, behind log-ins, or individually to season ticket holders (e.g. Sunderland, Fulham), so some figures rely on press reports.
  • Clubs often sell a few restricted or family-area seats below their usual entry price; compare notes, not just numbers.
  • Matchday prices depend on the fixture category, which clubs set match by match.

Corrections

If a price is wrong or out of date, please send the correct figure with a link to the club’s page. Contact details will be added before launch.
