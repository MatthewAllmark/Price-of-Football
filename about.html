About Price of Football & our methodology

Price of Football is an independent, unaffiliated database of what it costs to watch UK football. It covers all 116 clubs in the top five English divisions for the 2026/27 season: 20 Premier League, 24 Championship, 24 League One, 24 League Two, 24 National League.

Independent and unaffiliated

We are not connected to, sponsored by or endorsed by the Premier League, the EFL, the National League, any club, any ticket seller or any brewer. Club and competition names are used only to identify them. We deliberately don’t use club badges, crests or logos.

Where the numbers come from

  1. Club websites first — official ticketing pages, price lists and announcements.
  2. Reputable press second — national and local newspapers (e.g. BBC Sport, The Mirror, Manchester Evening News, Liverpool Echo, Birmingham Mail, Daily Echo, Lancashire Evening Post, Halifax Courier, Bristol Live, Peterborough Telegraph, The Star and other local titles) and GiveMeSport, where the club’s own figures couldn’t be read.
  3. Other sources only when labelled — a small number of figures come from fan sites, a ticket-guide site, a programme-collector site, a local news aggregator or a regional website republishing a price survey, or the fan guide site Football Ground Guide (used only where its page is dated and gives 2026/27 prices). These are tagged on each club page (e.g. “other”) so you can judge them.

League membership, stadium names and capacities are taken from Wikipedia’s 2026–27 Premier League, EFL Championship, League One, League Two and National League articles (which cite club and league sources). Premier League pint and pie prices come from a single press survey in which the newspaper contacted every club, so they’re comparable but are not club-published price lists.

League-specific notes

  • Premier League: adult away tickets are capped at £30 (extended for 2026/27 and 2027/28), so we show £30 for every ground.
  • EFL (Championship, League One, League Two): there is no away cap. Away prices come from the club, the visiting club’s published ticket details, or press reports of published research comparing every EFL club’s away-end prices; where a club prices by category we show the top tier and say so. Where that research gives only a club’s rank and not its price, we leave the price blank. Season-ticket figures mostly come from local-press comparisons and are often early-bird or renewal prices.
  • National League: most clubs publish one adult price, with discounts for buying in advance. Where a club charges more on the day, we show the advance price and give the on-the-day price in the note. Away prices come from the home club’s ticket page or the visiting club’s published ticket details.

Rules we follow

  • Every figure has a source link and the date we last checked it.
  • If we can’t find a figure from a source we trust, we show “—”. We never estimate, interpolate or guess.
  • Where sources disagree we use the club’s own figure if we can read it, otherwise the most specific press report, and we say so in the note.
  • Prices are adult, general admission and exclude hospitality unless the note says otherwise.

What each column means

  • Cheapest adult season ticket: Cheapest adult season ticket the club sells for home league games (see note for area/product).
  • Dearest adult season ticket: Most expensive adult general-admission season ticket (hospitality excluded where identifiable).
  • Cheapest adult matchday ticket: Cheapest adult single-match ticket for a league game (category/grade in note).
  • Dearest adult matchday ticket: Most expensive adult general-admission single-match ticket for a league game (category/grade in note).
  • Away ticket (adult, away end): Adult price paid by away fans. Premier League grounds: the league-wide £30 cap. Elsewhere: what the home club charges away fans (no cap; see note for tier/fixture).
  • Pint of lager: Price of a pint of lager inside the ground (club or reputable press survey; date in source).
  • Pie: Price of a pie inside the ground.
  • Matchday programme: Cover price of the matchday programme.

Known limitations

  • Several clubs publish prices only as images, behind log-ins, or individually to season ticket holders (e.g. Sunderland, Fulham), so some figures rely on press reports. Many EFL club sites block automated reading; a few figures were read from a search engine’s extract of the club page and are marked as such in the note.
  • Below the Premier League, coverage is uneven: we have season-ticket prices for most clubs but matchday prices for fewer, and only a handful of pint, pie or programme prices, taken from clubs’ own visitor guides, because we found no reputable 2026/27 survey for those leagues.
  • Early-bird, renewal and general-sale season-ticket prices differ; lower-league figures often reflect the early-bird or renewal rate, so the price a new fan pays now may be higher.
  • Clubs often sell a few restricted or family-area seats below their usual entry price; compare notes, not just numbers.
  • Matchday prices depend on the fixture category, which clubs set match by match.

The away day guide: where it comes from

Every club page has an away day guide for visiting supporters: where the away end is, the nearest station and how long the walk is, away-friendly pubs (with a Google Maps link) and pubs to avoid, parking and coach drop-off, food in the away end, and links to the club’s own visiting-supporter information. It currently covers 116 grounds and lists 341 away-friendly pubs and 89 pubs to avoid.

We use sources in this order, and every item carries a tag saying which kind it is:

  • Official — the home club’s own visiting-supporters page or away guide (or, where named, a public body such as a council or transport authority).
  • Other club’s guide — an official away guide published by a visiting club about this ground, shown as “From the X away guide”. These are usually written for one fixture, so details such as parking prices can be fixture-specific.
  • Supporters’ trust — a supporters’ trust or supporters’ club.
  • Press — reputable newspapers.
  • Fan guide — independent fan guides such as Football Ground Guide or Away Day Pubs, used mainly for pubs and only where the page says so.

Each item links to its source and shows the date we checked it. If a source is a guide from the 2024-25 season or earlier, we still show it but say “From the 2024-25 guide, check before you travel.” If a pub has closed, or a source says it is now home-only, we drop it or move it to “pubs to avoid”. We only include a pub, station, stand or walking time if a page we actually read says so — we never invent or estimate them, and if we couldn’t find something the section is simply left out. We don’t link to betting, ticket-resale or affiliate sites.

Pub policies change, so check before you travel. Colours rules may apply, and police can change arrangements for high-risk fixtures at short notice. The full data is available as away_guide.json.

Corrections

If a price is wrong or out of date, please send the correct figure with a link to the club’s page. Contact details will be added before launch.
