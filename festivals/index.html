How much does a UK festival cost in 2027?

Among the 45 festivals where we found a sourced 2027 price, the median cheapest adult full-festival ticket is £206.11. The cheapest is Big Sky Beer Festival at £32 and the dearest is Glastonbury Festival at £408. Booking fees, parking, campervan passes and refundable litter bonds can add a lot, so each festival page lists them where the festival publishes them.

Use the on-sale calendar to see which festivals are on sale, sold out or coming soon, and the budget calculator to add travel, food and drink to the ticket price.

Price of Festivals is an independent project. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or connected to any festival, promoter or ticket agent. Festival names are used for identification only.

Prices are collected by hand from festival websites and official ticket pages, and every figure links to its source with the date we checked it. Prices change by tier and sell out. Where we could not find a figure from a source we trust we show “—”; we never estimate. Always check the official seller before you buy, and never buy from unofficial resellers.
