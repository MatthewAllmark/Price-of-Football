About and methodology

Price of Festivals is a free, independent comparison of UK festival ticket prices. It is part of Price of Football, which tracks what it costs to watch football in the UK.

How prices are collected

Each figure is read by hand from the festival's own website, its official ticket page or an official ticket agent. Where only the press has published a price, we label it “Press”. Every figure shows its source link and the date we checked it. If we cannot find a 2027 price from a source we trust, we show “—”. We never estimate, carry over last year's price or fill gaps with averages.

Definitions

  • adult: Cheapest adult weekend (main full-festival) ticket on sale, or last on sale, for the 2027 edition. For camping festivals this is the ticket that includes camping. Partial-weekend tickets are listed as extras.
  • noncamp: Cheapest adult full-festival ticket without camping.
  • day: Cheapest adult day ticket.
  • per_day: adult price divided by the number of days the ticket covers: the days named in the ticket label where it covers fewer days than the festival, otherwise the calendar days in the published festival dates (computed, not sourced).
  • fees: included = price shown includes booking fee; extra = page says fees are added; unknown = page does not say.

Source types

  • Official: Festival's own site or its official ticket page
  • Ticket agent: An official ticket agent's listing
  • Press: Reputable press
  • Guide: Festival guide or listings site

Known limits

  • Festivals sell in tiers, so the cheapest tier may already have gone up or sold out by the time you read this.
  • Ticket agents add booking fees in different ways. We record whether the shown price includes them (“incl. booking fee”), says they are extra (“+ booking fee”) or does not say (“fees not stated”).
  • Some festivals show prices only inside a booking widget we could not read. These are listed with a blank price until we can confirm one.

Corrections

Spotted a wrong, missing or out-of-date price? Email corrections@example.com with the festival, the correct figure and a link to where it is published. We fix sourced corrections quickly.

How the site is funded

The site is free. It may in future carry clearly labelled sponsor slots, or affiliate links to official ticket agents and camping retailers, which would pay us a small commission at no extra cost to you. Affiliate links will never change which festivals appear or how they are ranked.

Data

The full dataset is free to download as JSON or CSV.

Get on-sale alerts

One short email when early birds drop, tiers go up or festivals sell out. No spam.
