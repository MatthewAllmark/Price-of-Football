Derby County ticket prices 2026/27

The cheapest adult Derby County season ticket for 2026/27 is £472.50 and the dearest we found is £610.50. Adult matchday tickets range from £26.00 to £34.00. A pint at Pride Park Stadium costs £6.00 and a pie £6.00. Away fans pay £26.00 for an adult ticket (there is no away price cap outside the Premier League).
