Arsenal ticket prices 2026/27

The cheapest adult Arsenal season ticket for 2026/27 is £978 and the dearest we found is £2,196. Adult matchday tickets range from £31.80 to £145.50. A pint at Emirates Stadium costs £7.00 and a pie £6.95. Away fans pay no more than £30 under the Premier League cap.
