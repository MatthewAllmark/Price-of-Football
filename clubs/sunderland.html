Sunderland ticket prices 2026/27

The cheapest adult Sunderland season ticket for 2026/27 is £550 and the dearest we found is £715. We have not yet found a reliable adult matchday ticket price range. A pint at Stadium of Light costs £4.95 and a pie £5.20. Away fans pay no more than £30 under the Premier League cap.

Parking and coach drop-off

  • Away coaches: Up to 24 supporter coaches can be booked in advance.
    Official · Sunderland · checked 29 Oct 2026 From the 2024-25 guide, check before you travel.
  • Matchday parking: Extremely limited stadium parking and a strictly enforced residents zone. Options: Stadium of Light Metro station car park, the Enterprise Park park and ride, or Wearmouth Colliery Welfare (free).
    Supporters' trust · Fulham Supporters' Trust · checked 29 Oct 2026
  • Park and ride: Park and Ride on Wessington Way (A1231), signposted from the A19; buses every five minutes from 90 minutes before kick-off, returning from Keir Hardie Way.
    Official · Sunderland · checked 29 Oct 2026 From the 2024-25 guide, check before you travel.
