  • Matchday parking: Limited ground parking, £10, first come first served. Or NCP Birmingham Airport Cargo Terminal (B26 3QT), £6 for up to 5 hours with code MOORS26 at the pay machine, 15-20 minutes walk. Parking on Damson Parkway is prohibited.
