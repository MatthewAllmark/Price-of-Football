Sheffield Wednesday ticket prices 2026/27

The cheapest adult Sheffield Wednesday season ticket for 2026/27 is £415 and the dearest we found is £600. Adult matchday tickets cost up to £46.00; we have not yet found a reliable cheapest price. Away fans pay £34.00 for an adult ticket (there is no away price cap outside the Premier League).

Away day guide: Hillsborough Stadium

Pub policies change, so check before you travel. Colours rules may apply.

Away end

West Stand (Leppings Lane), accessed via Leppings Lane

Nine wheelchair bays for visitors; accessible entrance next to turnstiles 14a-16a. Bags larger than A4 refused.

  • Leppings Lane (Supertram, Yellow route) (Tram) — About 5 minutes
