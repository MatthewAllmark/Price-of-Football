Coventry City ticket prices 2026/27

Away day guide: Coventry Building Society Arena

Pub policies change, so check before you travel. Colours rules may apply.

Away end

South Stand (typically Blocks 6-9; safe standing in Blocks 6-7)

Turnstiles open 90 minutes before kick-off. Safe standing in Blocks 6 and 7. After the match away fans exit across Car Park B, then left towards Arena Shopping Park and the station.

Other club's guide · From the Charlton Athletic away guide · checked 29 Oct 2026

Getting there

  • Coventry Arena (Rail) — On site
    Coventry Arena station is next to the stadium (normal timetable). Coventry main station is about 4 miles away; a £5 return matchday shuttle bus runs from it.
