Wolverhampton Wanderers ticket prices 2026/27

The cheapest adult Wolverhampton Wanderers season ticket for 2026/27 is £549 and the dearest we found is £699. Adult matchday tickets range from £20.00 to £41.50. A pint at Molineux Stadium costs £6.50 and a pie £5.00. Away fans pay £35.50 for an adult ticket (there is no away price cap outside the Premier League).
