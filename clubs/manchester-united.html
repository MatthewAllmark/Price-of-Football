Away day guide: Old Trafford

Pub policies change, so check before you travel. Colours rules may apply.

Away end

South-east corner, between the Sir Bobby Charlton (South) Stand and the East Stand

Allocation: About 3,000 for league matches (up to 9,000 for cups)

Around 2,500 safe-standing spaces in the away end. Turnstiles open two hours before kick-off; the concourse is cramped. Bags bigger than 20 x 15 x 5 cm must be stored (£5).
