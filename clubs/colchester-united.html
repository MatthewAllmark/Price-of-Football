Colchester United ticket prices 2026/27

The cheapest adult Colchester United season ticket for 2026/27 is £355 and the dearest we found is £488. Adult matchday tickets range from £19.25 to £37.00. Away fans pay £24.75 for an adult ticket (there is no away price cap outside the Premier League).

Getting there

  • Colchester — About 45 minutes
    Via Mile End Road, Nayland Road and Boxted Road, or a Park and Ride bus to within 10 minutes walk (buses stop before the end of midweek games).

Parking and coach drop-off

  • Matchday parking: Stadium car park (almost 700 spaces), typically £7.50, book in advance via colutickets.com (ANPR barrier).
    Official · Colchester United · checked 29 Oct 2026
