Cardiff City ticket prices 2026/27

The cheapest adult Cardiff City season ticket for 2026/27 is £294 and the dearest we found is £584. Adult matchday tickets range from £23.00 to £46.00. Away fans pay £32.00 for an adult ticket (there is no away price cap outside the Premier League).
