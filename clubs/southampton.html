Southampton ticket prices 2026/27

The cheapest adult Southampton season ticket for 2026/27 is £478 and the dearest we found is £827. Adult matchday tickets start at £30.00; we have not yet found a reliable top price. Away fans pay £40.00 for an adult ticket (there is no away price cap outside the Premier League).

Away day guide: St Mary's Stadium

Pub policies change, so check before you travel. Colours rules may apply.

Away end

Chapel / Kingsland corner (new away end); enter via Marine Parade if seated in the Chapel Stand

This is the current away end. Older fan guides still say the Northam Stand, which was the previous away end; check your ticket.

Official · Southampton · checked 29 Oct 2026

Parking and coach drop-off

  • Away coaches: Free parking for official/private away coaches and minibuses on Melbourne Street; book via Hampshire Police (PC Lee Grist).
    Official · Southampton · checked 29 Oct 2026
  • Matchday parking: No general parking onsite; use nearby city car parks. Shuttle bus from Ocean Village car park every 20 minutes to Britannia Road.
