Hull City ticket prices 2026/27

The cheapest adult Hull City season ticket for 2026/27 is £396 and the dearest we found is £600. Adult matchday tickets start at £30.00; we have not yet found a reliable top price. A pint at MKM Stadium costs £6.30 and a pie £5.75. Away fans pay no more than £30 under the Premier League cap.
