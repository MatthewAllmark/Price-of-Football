Queens Park Rangers ticket prices 2026/27

The cheapest adult Queens Park Rangers season ticket for 2026/27 is £275 and the dearest we found is £749. Adult matchday tickets range from £24.00 to £53.00. Away fans pay £41.00 for an adult ticket (there is no away price cap outside the Premier League).
