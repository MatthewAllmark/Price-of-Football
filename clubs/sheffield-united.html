Sheffield United ticket prices 2026/27

The cheapest adult Sheffield United season ticket for 2026/27 is £449 and the dearest we found is £559. Adult matchday tickets range from £25.00 to £41.00. Away fans pay £33.00 for an adult ticket (there is no away price cap outside the Premier League).

Away end

Bramall Lane Stand

Visiting supporters are normally in the Bramall Lane Stand. Turnstiles open about 90 minutes before kick-off; no cash turnstiles.

Official · Sheffield United · checked 29 Oct 2026

Getting there

  • Sheffield (Rail) — A few minutes (club guide); fan guides say 15-20 minutes
    Shoreham Street, cross St Mary's Road, left into Bramall Lane; away turnstiles on the left.
