Liverpool ticket prices 2026/27

The cheapest adult Liverpool season ticket for 2026/27 is £734.50 and the dearest we found is £931. Adult matchday tickets range from £9.00 to £62.75. A pint at Anfield costs £4.80 and a pie £4.50. Away fans pay no more than £30 under the Premier League cap.
