Away day guide: Spotland Stadium

Pub policies change, so check before you travel. Colours rules may apply.

Away end

Willbutts Lane Stand

Allocation: Typically about 1,500, towards the Pearl Street end; the whole stand (roughly 3,500) can be opened for big followings

Single tier, no pillars, runs the full length of the pitch.

Supporters' trust · Dale Trust (Rochdale supporters' trust) · checked 29 Oct 2026

Getting there

  • Rochdale (Rail) — About 30 minutes
    Taxi from the station about £8. Metrolink runs from Manchester to Rochdale Town Centre, about 20 minutes walk.
    Official · Rochdale · checked 29 Oct 2026

Away-friendly pubs

  • Rochdale Cricket Club — Bridgefold Road, under ten minutes' walk, free parking. Listed by the Dale Trust as welcoming away fans.

Parking and coach drop-off

  • Matchday parking: Stadium car park is permit holders only. Free parking at Rochdale Cricket Club (Bridgefold Road, OL11 5BX) and Spotland Bridge Mill (Mellor Street, OL11 5BU); Oulder Hill School is about 10 minutes away.
    Supporters' trust · Dale Trust (Rochdale supporters' trust) · checked 29 Oct 2026

Food and drink

  • Inside: burgers, hot dogs, pies and chips. A fish and chip shop is directly opposite the Willbutts Lane away entrance.
