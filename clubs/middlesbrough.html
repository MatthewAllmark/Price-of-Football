Middlesbrough ticket prices 2026/27

The cheapest adult Middlesbrough season ticket for 2026/27 is £540 and the dearest we found is £701. Adult matchday tickets range from £29.50 to £40.50. Away fans pay £33.00 for an adult ticket (there is no away price cap outside the Premier League).
