West Ham United ticket prices 2026/27

The cheapest adult West Ham United season ticket for 2026/27 is £310 and the dearest we found is £1,200. Adult matchday tickets range from £25.00 to £55.00. Away fans pay £30.00 for an adult ticket (there is no away price cap outside the Premier League).

Away day guide: London Stadium

Pub policies change, so check before you travel. Colours rules may apply.

Away end

Away section reached via Bridge 4 to Turnstile D (lift access)

Area outside becomes away-only after the match.

Official · West Ham United · checked 29 Oct 2026

Getting there

  • Pudding Mill Lane — About 10 minutes
    Bridge 4 has lift access straight to Turnstile D. Stratford, Stratford International, Hackney Wick and Stratford High Street are all about 20 minutes.
    Official · West Ham United · checked 29 Oct 2026

Away-friendly pubs

Pub names link to Google Maps.

  • The Sportsman — The strongest nearby lead, around 15-18 minutes walk. Very few pubs near the stadium reliably admit away fans.
    Fan guide · Away Day Pubs · checked 29 Oct 2026
  • Hamilton Hall — Liverpool Street; home and away friendly, around 20 minutes by train and walk.
    Fan guide · Away Day Pubs · checked 29 Oct 2026

Pubs to avoid

Listed by the sources below as home-only or not for visiting fans.

Parking and coach drop-off

  • Away coaches: Coach parking arranged between clubs; contact West Ham supporter services.
    Official · West Ham United · checked 29 Oct 2026
  • Matchday parking: No public car parking at London Stadium; six accessible bays for away supporters (contact the club). Stratford and Stratford International stations are about 20 minutes walk.
    Official · West Ham United · checked 29 Oct 2026

Food and drink

  • Range of food and drink outlets inside and outside the stadium. Cashless, including programme sellers.
