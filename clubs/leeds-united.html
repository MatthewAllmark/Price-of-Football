Away day guide: Elland Road

Pub policies change, so check before you travel. Colours rules may apply.

Away end

South West corner, John Charles Stand: upper tier (yellow tickets, Entrances 1-2) and lower tier (black tickets, via Howards entrance)

Enter through the blue gates in the South West corner; away fan zone beside the West Stand car park.

Official · Leeds United · checked 29 Oct 2026

Getting there

  • Leeds
    Matchday R2 bus from Sovereign Street (outside Caffe Nero) from 90 minutes before kick-off; MCards and Day Rovers are not valid on it.
    Official · Leeds United · checked 29 Oct 2026

Away-friendly pubs

Pub names link to Google Maps.

  • Howards Bar — Lower-tier (black ticket) entrance, also open to visiting supporters for a drink, from 90 minutes before kick-off. The away fan zone outside the away turnstiles opens 2 hours before kick-off. The club says no pubs near the stadium accept visiting supporters; several city-centre pubs do.
    Official · Leeds United · checked 29 Oct 2026

Parking and coach drop-off

  • Away coaches: Away coach park on Fullerton Car Park; stewards direct you through the away access tunnel to the away turnstiles.
    Official · Leeds United · checked 29 Oct 2026
  • Matchday parking: Leeds City Council car parks next to the stadium (Car Parks A and B, Fullerton, Elland Road Park and Ride), £12 per car, cash only.
    Official · Leeds United · checked 29 Oct 2026

Food and drink

  • Food and drink kiosks around the stadium; alcohol at every kiosk but not in the seating bowl. Cashless.
    Official · Leeds United · checked 29 Oct 2026
