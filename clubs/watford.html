Watford ticket prices 2026/27

The cheapest adult Watford season ticket for 2026/27 is £443 and the dearest we found is £578. Adult matchday tickets range from £33.00 to £41.00. Away fans pay £36.00 for an adult ticket (there is no away price cap outside the Premier League).
