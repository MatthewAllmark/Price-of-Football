Ipswich Town ticket prices 2026/27

The cheapest adult Ipswich Town season ticket for 2026/27 is £469 and the dearest we found is £747. Adult matchday tickets range from £28.00 to £48.00. A pint at Portman Road costs £5.80 and a pie £6.75. Away fans pay no more than £30 under the Premier League cap.

Away end

Cobbold Stand: VU1-VU3 (safe standing, upper tier) plus VL and VA (seated)

Turnstiles open 90 minutes before kick-off. No lift to the upper tier. Accessible entrance to the left of the away turnstiles.

Parking and coach drop-off

  • Away coaches: All away coaches park in the West End Road car park, a short walk from the away pub and the stadium.
    Official · Ipswich Town · checked 29 Oct 2026
  • Matchday parking: Large car park on Portman Road (IP1 2BP) right by the away end; event-day charge £15 at the council car parks. Expect to wait 30-45 minutes to leave after the game.
    Official · Ipswich Town · checked 29 Oct 2026

Food and drink
