Manchester City ticket prices 2026/27

The cheapest adult Manchester City season ticket for 2026/27 is £425 and the dearest we found is £1,600. Adult matchday tickets range from £20.00 to £60.00. A pint at Etihad Stadium costs £6.35 and a pie £5.25. Away fans pay no more than £30 under the Premier League cap.
