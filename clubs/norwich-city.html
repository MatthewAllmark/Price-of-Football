Norwich City ticket prices 2026/27

The cheapest adult Norwich City season ticket for 2026/27 is £567.50 and the dearest we found is £950. Adult matchday tickets range from £27.00 to £46.00. Away fans pay £37.00 for an adult ticket (there is no away price cap outside the Premier League).
