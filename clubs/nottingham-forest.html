Away day guide: City Ground

Pub policies change, so check before you travel. Colours rules may apply.

Away end

John Robertson Stand (formerly the Bridgford Stand), away section, turnstiles 41-53

Turnstiles usually open 90 minutes before kick-off. Visitors' ticket office collection point is next to the away turnstiles behind the Bridgford Stand. The stand was renamed from Bridgford to John Robertson; recent fan guides put visitors in its lower tier.

Official · Nottingham Forest · checked 29 Oct 2026
