Blackburn Rovers ticket prices 2026/27

The cheapest adult Blackburn Rovers season ticket for 2026/27 is £299 and the dearest we found is £499. Adult matchday tickets cost up to £45.00; we have not yet found a reliable cheapest price. Away fans pay £33.00 for an adult ticket (there is no away price cap outside the Premier League).
