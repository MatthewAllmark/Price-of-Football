Away day guide: Gander Green Lane

Pub policies change, so check before you travel. Colours rules may apply.

Away end

Collingwood Road End, via Collingwood Recreation Ground (Gate B), turnstiles 5-8

Turnstiles open at 1pm on Saturdays, 6pm midweek. Away ticket office by turnstiles 5-8 (card only).

Official · Sutton United · checked 29 Oct 2026

Getting there

  • West Sutton (Rail) — Next to the ground
    Turn right into Gander Green Lane, walk past the main entrance and after about 70 yards turn right into Collingwood Recreation Ground for turnstiles 5-8.
    Official · Sutton United · checked 29 Oct 2026
  • Sutton (Rail) — About 20 minutes (1.3 miles)
    The club's recommended station for visiting fans. Buses 413 (Gander Green Lane) and 80 (Collingwood Road) also run close by.
    Official · Sutton United · checked 29 Oct 2026
