Away day guide: Villa Park

Pub policies change, so check before you travel. Colours rules may apply.

Away end

North East corner of the Doug Ellis Stand: Lower Block Q and Upper Block P

Allocation: Approx. 1,600 (Lower Q) + 1,300 (Upper P) = c.2,900

Upper tier turnstiles P87, P88, P88A and P89 (64 steps up); lower tier Q91 to Q96B. Alcohol is sold in the lower tier (Block Q) only, and not at every match.

Official · Aston Villa · checked 29 Oct 2026

Parking and coach drop-off

  • Away coaches: Free away coach park on Witton Lane, close to the away turnstiles and opposite the visitors' ticket office. Accessible parking for visiting disabled fans is on the away coach park, by permit bought in advance from your club.
    Official · Aston Villa · checked 29 Oct 2026
  • Matchday parking: Use M6 junction 6; parking at Powerleague, Lichfield Road. Traffic exclusion zone on Witton Lane and Trinity Road from two hours before kick-off; residents' parking zones (£60 fines).
    Official · Aston Villa · checked 29 Oct 2026
  • Park and ride: Witton station (nearest to the away end) offers park and ride north of Villa Park on the Walsall line. A matchday shuttle bus runs from Dudley Street near New Street station.
    Official · Aston Villa · checked 29 Oct 2026
