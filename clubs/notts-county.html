Notts County ticket prices 2026/27

The cheapest adult Notts County season ticket for 2026/27 is £450 and the dearest we found is £495. Adult matchday tickets cost up to £30.00; we have not yet found a reliable cheapest price. Away fans pay £26.00 for an adult ticket (there is no away price cap outside the Premier League).
