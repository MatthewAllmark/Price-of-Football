Millwall ticket prices 2026/27

The cheapest adult Millwall season ticket for 2026/27 is £482 and the dearest we found is £622. Adult matchday tickets range from £31.00 to £44.00. A pint at The Den costs £7.20 and a pie £5.75. Away fans pay £34.00 for an adult ticket (there is no away price cap outside the Premier League).
