Newcastle United ticket prices 2026/27

The cheapest adult Newcastle United season ticket for 2026/27 is £380 and the dearest we found is £987. Adult matchday tickets range from £35.00 to £80.00. A pint at St James' Park costs £5.85 and a pie £6.10. Away fans pay no more than £30 under the Premier League cap.
