Away day guide: Turf Moor

Pub policies change, so check before you travel. Colours rules may apply.

Away end

Barnfield Construction Stand; visiting fans arrive via Belvedere Road

Home and away entrances were swapped after recent works. Turnstiles open 90 minutes before kick-off; bag searches and dogs at entry.

Official · Burnley · checked 29 Oct 2026

Getting there

  • Burnley Manchester Road (Rail) — About 15 minutes
    Walk down Centenary Way, right at the roundabout onto Yorkshire Street, which becomes Harry Potts Way; Turf Moor is on the left. Burnley Central is closer but has fewer trains.
    Official · Burnley · checked 29 Oct 2026

Parking and coach drop-off

  • Away coaches: Organised supporter coaches drop off and park on Belvedere Road (inside the road closure), a short walk via Ormerod Yard to the away turnstiles.
    Official · Burnley · checked 29 Oct 2026
  • Matchday parking: No on-site parking for visiting fans. Options include Burnley Cricket Club on Belvedere Road (about 2 minutes), Townley Park (about 10 minutes), Charter Walk (500 spaces, about 8 minutes) and the council car park on Centenary Way (about 5 minutes).
    Official · Burnley · checked 29 Oct 2026

Food and drink

  • Cashless stadium. A marquee to the right of the away turnstiles sells food and drink. Alcohol may be restricted or suspended for some fixtures.
    Official · Burnley · checked 29 Oct 2026
